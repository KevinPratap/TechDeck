# Code of Conduct

We want PocketDeck to be a friendly, useful place for everyone who uses it or helps build it.

**Expected:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and accept feedback gracefully, and keep discussion about the work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults or personal attacks, discrimination of any kind, publishing other people's private information, and spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile.

This is a short version in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
